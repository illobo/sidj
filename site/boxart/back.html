MUSIC TRACKER · LIVE SEQUENCER

COMPOSE

Write phrases of 16 steps, string them into chains, and build the song from chains. Three SID voices, six with a second SID. Instruments with wave, pulse and filter tables. 32 factory presets and drum kits.

PERFORM

Live mode queues the next chain on each channel at the end of the current one. Mute, solo, tap tempo and nudge. Filter, resonance and volume faders on screen.

DRUM LANES

Eight drum sounds side by side, sixteen steps across. Set a hit with SPACE or a click of the mouse.

SLICE

T.A.S.T.E. plays a .SID tune and writes a slice of it as a SIDJ song: notes, instruments and timing, ready to rework.
REQUIRES
Commodore 64 or C64 Ultimate
1541-compatible disk drive
OPTIONAL
1351 mouse in port 1
RAM Expansion Unit: undo history, song slots, crash recovery, drum lanes
DISK CONTAINS: SIDJ · TASTE
Manual and quick reference card enclosed.
