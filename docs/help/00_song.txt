SONG
256 rows 00-FF, one column per channel.
A field holds a chain 00-7F or --.

{SPACE,SPACE} on --  new chain
{SPACE} on --        the last chain
{SPACE+L/R}  -1/+1   {SPACE+U/D}  +-10
{C=+SPACE}  cut      {CTRL+SPACE}  paste
{RETURN}             play from row / stop
{CTRL+RETURN}        play all / stop
{C=+U/D}             page 16 rows, or the
                     next bookmark
{C=,C=,C=}           bookmark on / off
{CTRL+LEFT}          live mode on / off
{CTRL+RIGHT}         open the chain
{CTRL+C=}  mark      {F1 F3 F5}  mute
{F7}  undo           {SHIFT+F7}  redo
