GRID VIEW AND DRUM VIEW
grid ({CLR/HOME}): the steps as 4x4 boxes
{CRSR}            move, {U/D}: 4 steps
{C=+L/R}          field: note, instr,
                  command, value
{SPACE} {SPACE+CRSR} {C=+SPACE} as in the
list. Mouse: click a step on / off,
drag {U/D} note, {L/R} instrument,
right drag: command value.
drum view ({SHIFT+CLR/HOME}, REU):
8 lanes (instruments) x 16 steps
{L/R}  step       {U/D}  lane
{SPACE}           hit on / off
{C=+SPACE}        clear the step
{SPACE+U/D}       hit +-1 semitone
{C=+U/D}          shift the lanes
{CLR/HOME}        back to the list
