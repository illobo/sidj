CHAIN
16 steps: a phrase 00-FE and a
transpose. The chain ends at the first
empty step.

{SPACE,SPACE} on --  new phrase
{SPACE+L/R}  -1/+1   {SPACE+U/D}  +-10
transpose: {SPACE+L/R} semitone,
           {SPACE+U/D} +-12
{C=+U/D}             first / last step
{RETURN}             play / stop channel
{CTRL+RIGHT}         open the phrase
{CTRL+LEFT}          back to SONG
{CTRL+C=}  mark      {C=}  copy block
{CTRL+SPACE}         paste
CTRL held: {C=,SPACE} clones the phrase
                     (PROJECT: CLONE)
