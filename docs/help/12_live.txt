LIVE MODE (SONG SCREEN)
{CTRL+LEFT}  live mode on / off
each channel loops its chain; a queued
chain starts when the playing one ends.
{RETURN}             queue the chain
{RETURN,RETURN}      at the phrase end
{CTRL+RETURN}        queue a stop
                     (twice: phrase end)
hold {LEFT}, {RETURN}  queue the row
> playing  * queued  X stop queued
{F1 F3 F5}           mute 1 2 3
hold {C=}, {CTRL}    mute cursor channel
{C=+RETURN}          solo, again: all on
mouse: click a chain: queue, twice:
phrase end, right click: stop.
HFF in a phrase stops the channel.
